Privacy Policy

At AromaticRoots, we value your trust and respect your privacy. This policy explains how information may be collected, used, protected, and managed when you visit our spice and condiments store.

Last Updated: October 12, 2026
01

Introduction

Welcome to AromaticRoots. We are committed to respecting your privacy and handling your personal information responsibly. This policy describes the information practices associated with our website, products, enquiry forms, and customer communications.

02

Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with our website, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, enquiry details, product interests, and order-related information. Basic technical information, such as browser type and website usage data, may also be collected where applicable.

03

How We Use Your Information

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, provide product details, process orders, communicate important updates, improve website functionality, and provide customer support. We aim to use personal information for legitimate business purposes and as permitted by applicable law.

04

Enquiry Form Privacy

Details submitted through our enquiry form may be used to understand your requirements and respond to your request. Please provide accurate information and avoid including unnecessary sensitive details. Enquiry information should only be accessed by authorised personnel or relevant service providers who need it to assist you.

05

Products, Spices and Recipes

Our website may display spice blends, condiments, ingredients, cooking tips, and recipes. Information you share when asking about products or recipes may be used to answer your questions and improve our services. Product descriptions and recipes are provided for general informational purposes.

06

Cookies and Website Preferences

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support essential functionality, remember preferences, understand website usage, or improve your browsing experience. You can manage cookies through your browser settings, although disabling certain cookies may affect some website features.

07

Sharing of Information

We do not intend to sell your personal information. Information may be shared with authorised service providers supporting website hosting, communications, order fulfilment, or necessary business operations. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or to protect legitimate rights and safety.

08

Data Security

We take reasonable measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or alteration. However, no website, electronic transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Please exercise care when sharing information online.

09

Data Retention

Personal information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, fulfil orders, maintain business records, resolve disputes, and comply with legal obligations. When information is no longer needed, reasonable steps may be taken to delete it or securely dispose of it.

10

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may be able to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or raise concerns about how it is handled. You may also have the right to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Identity verification may be required.

11

Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external websites or services for convenience and additional information. These third parties may have their own privacy policies and practices. AromaticRoots is not responsible for the privacy, content, or security practices of websites that we do not operate.

12

Policy Updates and Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy when our practices, services, or legal requirements change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date where appropriate. For questions or privacy-related requests, please contact our team.
